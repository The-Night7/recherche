Is your CV failing

January is a peak time for 
workers to consider seeking 
out a new job, with back-to-
work blues driving many of us

But is your résumé letting

employers are looking for .
A survey of 2,000

480 recruiters, conducted by 
Mortar London for Michael 
Page, has compared what 
each group thinks is vital.
