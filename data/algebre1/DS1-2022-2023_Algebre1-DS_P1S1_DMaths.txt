Démontrer que :
1. A = (A ∪ B) ∩ (A ∪ B c).
2. A \ (B ∪ C ) = (A \ B) ∩ (A \ C ).

1. 1 point.
A = A ∪ ; = A ∪ (B ∩ B c) = A ∪ (B ∩ B c) = (A ∪ B) ∩ (A ∪ B c)
